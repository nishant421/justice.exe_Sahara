# Support Resources — Helplines, One Stop Centres, Counseling

## Childline 1098 — Child Protection Helpline

Childline 1098 is India's first 24-hour, free, emergency phone outreach service for children in need of care and protection. It is available 365 days a year, free of cost from any phone (mobile or landline). Childline can be called by: the child themselves; a parent, guardian, or relative; a neighbour, teacher, or concerned citizen; any person who has witnessed or suspects child abuse. Childline is operated by trained social workers and counsellors who respond to the call within minutes. Childline does not disclose the identity of the caller or the child without consent.

## What Childline Does After a Call

When a call is made to 1098, Childline staff: immediately assess the severity (emergency or non-emergency); for emergencies, dispatch a Childline team or coordinate with police and SJPU within minutes; provide counselling and emotional support over the phone; connect the child and family to shelter homes, medical care, legal aid, and rehabilitation services; follow up to ensure the child's safety over time. Childline maintains strict confidentiality. No call to Childline is considered too small — they handle everything from abuse to runaways to children in need.

## iCALL — Psychological Counseling Service

iCALL is a free, confidential psychological counselling and referral helpline run by the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai. It provides free mental health support to survivors of abuse, harassment, trauma, and distressing life experiences. Contact: 9152987821. Service hours: Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 10 PM. Languages: Hindi and English. Services include: individual counselling sessions; crisis support; referrals to local mental health professionals; support for secondary trauma (for family members of survivors). Email: icall@tiss.edu. iCALL counsellors are trained, supervised professionals bound by confidentiality.

## One Stop Centres — Sakhi

One Stop Centres (OSCs), also called Sakhi Centres, provide integrated services to women affected by violence including domestic violence, sexual assault, workplace harassment, and child marriage. Services available at One Stop Centres: emergency shelter (up to 5 days); medical examination and treatment; police facilitation (FIR registration support); psycho-social support and counselling; legal aid and court support; video-conferencing facility for statement recording. Toll-free number: 181 (Women Helpline). One Stop Centres are located in district hospitals and medical colleges across India. They are operational 24 hours, 7 days a week.

## Women Helpline 181

The Women Helpline 181 is a toll-free, 24x7 helpline for women in distress. It connects callers to: the nearest One Stop Centre; police assistance; legal aid services; shelter homes; medical support. 181 also handles cases of workplace harassment, domestic violence, and child protection. Calls are confidential. 181 can dispatch teams for rescue in emergencies.

## iCALL and Mental Health Support for Survivors

Sexual abuse, harassment, and exploitation cause significant psychological trauma including PTSD, depression, anxiety, and self-harm. Seeking mental health support is an important part of recovery and is not a sign of weakness. iCALL (9152987821) provides: free counselling sessions without requiring prior appointment; support in processing trauma; guidance on coping strategies; referrals to local psychiatrists and mental health professionals if needed. Family members and secondary survivors (parents, siblings, friends) of abuse victims can also call iCALL for support.

## NALSA Legal Aid Helpline 15100

NALSA's toll-free helpline 15100 connects callers to free legal services including: a free legal aid lawyer for POCSO, POSH, and child marriage cases; guidance on how to file FIR, complaint, or legal petition; information on victim compensation; emergency legal assistance. The helpline operates through the District Legal Services Authorities. Callers are connected to their nearest DLSA. Legal aid is provided without any income test for women and child abuse victims.

## Emergency Contacts — Quick Reference

Police Emergency: 112 (available 24x7 across India). Childline: 1098 (child protection, 24x7 free). Women Helpline: 181 (women in distress, 24x7 free). iCALL Counseling: 9152987821 (Mon-Sat 8AM-10PM). NALSA Legal Aid: 15100 (free legal services). SHe-Box (POSH complaints): shebox.nic.in. These are all free services. No one should hesitate to call because of cost. Reaching out for help is the first and most important step.

## What to Do If You Are In Immediate Danger

If you or someone you know is in immediate physical danger: Call 112 immediately — this is the police emergency number available across India. State your location clearly. If you cannot speak, press a button or leave the line open — police are trained to respond. Childline 1098 also handles emergency child rescue. One Stop Centre 181 can dispatch rescue teams. Do not wait — immediate danger requires immediate action. Your safety is the priority above all legal processes.
